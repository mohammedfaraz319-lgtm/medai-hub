Medical Diagnosis System Using AI & Machine Learning

Final-year project · Department of Computer Science & Engineering · Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU)

Abstract

The introduction of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning into healthcare is changing the quality, efficiency and delivery of medical services. This project presents a smart healthcare platform that uses AI and ML for disease prediction, clinical decision support and patient management. It processes patient data and diagnostic images, detects patterns, aids early diagnosis and suggests precautions and the right specialist - while keeping patient data private and secure.
